# Outreach targets

| Type | Where to find them | What we offer |
|---|---|---|
| Architecture colleges (TN, Karnataka) | COA approved list, college hub pages | A free NATA drawing workshop for their outreach days; a guest talk for school visits |
| College fest organisers (B.Arch fests) | Fest pages, Instagram | Sponsor a sketching competition prize; judge panel from Neram tutors |
| School career counsellors | Schools near each centre | A 45-minute "Is architecture for you?" session for Class 10 to 12, free |
| Architecture blogs and YouTubers | Search, YouTube | Data they can cite: the 10-year NATA cutoff trend page and the free tools |
| Education journalists (TN, Karnataka) | Bylines on NATA and counselling news | Comment on NATA and B.Arch counselling changes; cutoff data |
