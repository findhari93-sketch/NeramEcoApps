# Google review requests

Ask enrolled families for a Google review of **their** centre, twice: after week 3 of classes, and after results. Send from the centre's WhatsApp, one family at a time.

## Rules

- No incentives, no discounts for reviews, no review swaps, no staff or friends posing as students.
- Never write the review for them or suggest a star rating.
- Use the centre's own Google profile link from `NAP_MASTER.md`. Online-only students review the Bangalore HQ profile once it exists.
- A review on the site needs written consent first (Admin, Testimonials), which is separate from a Google review.

## Templates

### After week 3 (English)

> Hi {Parent name}, this is {Staff name} from Neram Classes {Centre}. {Student name} has finished three weeks with us. If the classes are helping, would you share a short Google review? It helps other families find us. {Google profile review link}
> If something is not working, reply here and we will fix it first.

## Tracking

Log each request (date, centre, family, sent by) in the CRM notes. Review counts per centre go in the monthly report.
