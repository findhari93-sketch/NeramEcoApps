OUR STORY

Architects first. Software architects too.

For bigger exams like JEE and NEET, students have countless platforms, apps, and tools. Architecture entrance preparation had almost none. The field was too niche for anyone to build proper digital infrastructure for it, so we built it ourselves.

We are architects first. We studied at IITs, NITs, and Schools of Planning and Architecture, and we design buildings and spaces in the real world. We are also software architects. We combined both crafts to build a digital ecosystem for NATA preparation that no other classroom in India has.

At the heart of it is Nexus, our virtual learning assistant. It is a pocket tutor that helps students learn for the exam anytime, anywhere. Built entirely around the student experience, it makes Neram the only AI-enabled learning platform created for architecture entrance preparation.

Mission

Architecture is a unique path, and most students who dream of it have no one around them who has walked it. They struggle alone to understand the field, crack the entrance exam, and reach a good college. Neram Classes exists to remove that struggle.

Vision

We see a future where any student who dreams of becoming an architect can crack the exam with a strong score, get into a great college, and build a bright career, no matter which town they come from or who they know.
