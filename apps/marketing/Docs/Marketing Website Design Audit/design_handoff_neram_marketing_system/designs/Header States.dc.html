Today the header changes size and colour with each account state: orange Join Now, blue Continue/Track, green Pay and Go to App. The Login button turns into an avatar, and My Enrollment and the bell appear and disappear. The redesign keeps four fixed slots. The primary slot is always one gold button, and the account status is a small dot plus the button's wording.

What breaks the brand today
  • White bar over a navy hero, with a Material-blue banner on top. That's three unrelated surfaces in the first 270px.
  • Orange Join Now (theme secondary) appears nowhere else on the site.
  • The split-pill colour flips to blue or green with application status (HeaderCtaPill.tsx).
  • Login is a plain text link. After sign-in it becomes a 36–40px avatar, and the gold-gradient ring for enrolled students is hard-coded.
  • My Enrollment and the bell arrive only for some states, so the nav reflows when you sign in.

Implementation notes

  • getCtaConfig and ctaTone collapse into one map: status → { label, short, href, dot }. Remove toneSx and the hard-coded greens.
  • HeaderCtaPill splits into two plain buttons. One shape per job reads clearer than a split pill whose halves differ in colour.
  • The approved pulse goes. The green dot and the "Pay & join" wording carry the urgency, and nothing moves for reduced-motion users.
  • Enrolled avatar: a solid gold.500 ring replaces GOLD_GRADIENT. My Enrollment takes the secondary slot, so the nav never reflows.
  • The account slot reserves 92px while auth loads, so the bar doesn't jump.
  • Below 1100px the secondary button shows its icon only (label in the tooltip), so all five nav groups fit down to 900px. Below 900px the phone bar takes over. Nav links are never clipped.
