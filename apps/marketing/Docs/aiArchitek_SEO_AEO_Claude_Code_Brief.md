Do not create copy such as:

> “Looking for I Arch? aiArchitek is the best alternative…”

Do not repeatedly insert `i arch`, `iarch`, competitor URLs, competitor names, or misspellings purely to manipulate rankings.

Do not hide competitor keywords in CSS, metadata, alt text, HTML comments, off-screen text or other invisible content.

Do not create city pages whose only difference is the city name.

The goal is **semantic relevance + real usefulness**, not keyword stuffing.

# 20. Important SEO Principle

Google's current guidance emphasizes people-first content rather than content created primarily to manipulate rankings. AI-generated content is not automatically disallowed, but using automation primarily to manipulate search rankings can violate spam policies.

Therefore:

**Use the Ads data to discover what students want.  
Use that intent to create genuinely useful content.  
Do not turn the Ads keywords into a keyword-stuffed competitor page.**
